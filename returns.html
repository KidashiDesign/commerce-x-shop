Returns

Our 30-day satisfaction guarantee, explained

30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

We want you to love your purchase. If you're not completely satisfied, you can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

How to Start a Return

Contact us via our contact page with your order number and the item(s) you'd like to return. We'll provide return instructions and, where applicable, a prepaid shipping label.

Return Conditions

  • Items must be unused and in their original packaging
  • Proof of purchase or order number is required
  • Returns must be initiated within 30 days of delivery

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we'll notify you of the approval status of your refund. Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5-10 business days.

Exchanges

Need a different size or model? Let us know when you start your return and we'll help arrange an exchange where stock allows.

Questions?

Reach out anytime via our contact page and we'll be happy to help.
