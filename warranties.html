Warranties

What's covered, and for how long

2-Year Limited Warranty

All Commerce X products come with a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship from the original date of purchase.

What's Covered

  • Manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship
  • Component failures under normal use

What's Not Covered

  • Damage from accidents, misuse, or unauthorized modifications
  • Normal wear and tear
  • Damage caused by improper use or failure to follow product instructions

How to Make a Warranty Claim

Contact us via our contact page with your order number and a description of the issue. We'll guide you through the repair, replacement, or refund process.

Questions?

If you're unsure whether your product is covered, get in touch through our contact page and we'll be happy to help.
