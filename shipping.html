Shipping Info

Everything you need to know about how we get your order to you

Shipping Options

  • Free Shipping: Orders over $100 USD
  • Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days
  • Express Shipping: 2-3 business days
  • International: 10-15 business days

Order Processing

Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days. You'll receive a confirmation email as soon as your order ships, including a tracking number so you can follow it right to your door.

International Shipping

We ship to over 100 countries worldwide. Please note that international orders may be subject to customs fees, duties, or taxes imposed by the destination country, which are the responsibility of the customer.

Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your location and the shipping method selected. Orders over $100 USD qualify for free standard shipping.

Questions?

If you have any questions about your shipment, feel free to reach out via our contact page.
